About

I'm a DevOps Engineer focused on deploying scalable, highly available infrastructure on AWS, with growing exposure to GCP and Azure. My day-to-day work spans Kubernetes, Terraform, Docker, and Helm, alongside CI/CD lifecycle automation across Jenkins, GitHub Actions, and GitLab CI/CD.

I bring DevSecOps fundamentals into pipelines with SonarQube, ship changes through GitOps delivery via Argo CD, and write reusable Terraform modules built for multi-cloud environments.
